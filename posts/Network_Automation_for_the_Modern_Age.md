Yesterday I gave a talk at [DENOG 18](https://www.denog.de/de/meetings/denog18/index.html)
called “Network Automation for the Modern Age”. Marcus Stögbauer and I gave a
primordial version of this talk at this year’s DKNOG, but since then much has
happened.

It also is the first official [cyberwitchery](https://blog.veitheller.de/cyberwitchery.html)
talk, and the recording is already up on
[media.ccc.de](https://media.ccc.de/v/denog18-9-network-automation-for-the-modern-age),
with a mirror on YouTube.

It’s about putting your own data model at the center of your network
automation, with [alembic](https://github.com/cyberwitchery/alembic) as the
case study and a live demo in the middle.

I hope you like it, I was honestly quite pleased with how it turned out.
If any of this sounds relevant to your network, or you’d like me to give a
version of the talk somewhere, [let us know](mailto:contact@cyberwitchery.com)!
